ABOUT IBIZAINTERX

Built around the
experience.

IbizaInterX started with a simple idea: planning an exceptional trip should be part of the excitement, not the complication.

We're bringing accommodation, dining, events and experiences together in one connected platform, shaped around each group, their preferences and the way they want to experience Ibiza.

The customer chooses what they want to experience. IbizaInterX works out the best way to deliver it.

Technology connects the details. People, places and experiences make the trip unforgettable.

WHY WE BUILT IT

Ibiza is incredible.
Planning it often isn’t.

Organising a group trip can quickly become a maze of WhatsApp conversations, different preferences, scattered bookings, changing availability and decisions about money.

We believe there's a better way.

IbizaInterX brings personalised discovery, intelligent budgeting, collaborative planning and coordinated booking requests into one place.

A clearer way to plan, make decisions and manage the details — so everyone can focus on enjoying the experience.

Less chasing.
More discovering.
More experiencing.

FOUNDER

Built from experience,
not assumption.

IbizaInterX is shaped by first-hand experience of Ibiza's travel and concierge market.

We've seen what makes the island extraordinary, the importance of local knowledge and trusted supplier relationships, and where the traditional customer experience can become unnecessarily complicated.

Our ambition isn't to replace the personal service that makes Ibiza special. It's to give that service a smarter foundation.

Combining technology, expertise and genuine connections to make every trip more considered, connected and effortless.
