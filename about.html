ABOUT IBIZAINTERX

Built around the
experience.

IbizaInterX started with a simple idea. Planning Ibiza should feel as exciting as the trip itself.

WHY WE BUILT IT

Ibiza is incredible.
Planning it often isn’t.

We know the Ibiza concierge market and we know what planning a trip can become. Endless WhatsApp messages, scattered bookings, changing availability and too much time spent trying to keep everything together.

It takes the buzz out of something that should be exciting.

IbizaInterX is our answer. A customer first platform that brings discovery, AI tailored itineraries, group planning and direct supplier booking into one place.

Less chasing.
More discovering.
More experiencing.

FOUNDER

Built from experience,
not assumption.

IbizaInterX comes from first hand experience of the Ibiza travel and concierge market, seeing both what makes the island special and where the customer experience can fall apart.

The goal is simple. Use technology to remove the friction without removing the people, suppliers and experiences that make Ibiza what it is.
